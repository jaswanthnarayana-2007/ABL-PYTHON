# Algorithm: Student Club Management

1. Start.
2. Read coding-club roll numbers separated by spaces.
3. Convert the entered roll numbers into a set to avoid duplicates.
4. Read sports-club roll numbers separated by spaces.
5. Convert those roll numbers into a set.
6. Display the members of each club.
7. Calculate and display:
   - Union: members in either club.
   - Intersection: members in both clubs.
   - Difference: members only in the coding club.
8. Read a roll number to search.
9. If the roll number is present in either set, display that the student belongs to at least one club.
10. Otherwise, display that the student does not belong to either club.
11. Stop.
